Who We Are

BLD Institute of Research & Learning Foundation

BLD Institute of Research & Learning (BLDIRL), an institution based in National Capital Region aims to popularize science and technology by organizing various outreach programs to generate interest and creativity for innovation among students and faculty through a dedicated team of top academicians, well known researchers and experts from the field science and technology.

Our Vision

To foster a progressive, inclusive, and empowered society that enables lifelong learning, personal growth, and equal opportunities, empowering people and communities to contribute meaningfully to sustainable social and economic development.

Our Mission

To provide academic support and learning opportunities to help learners overcome educational gaps. To enhance employability through skill development, vocational training, and career readiness programs.
